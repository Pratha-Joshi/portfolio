EXP-01

Customer Review Retrieval & Evidence-Based Analytics

An end-to-end information retrieval and RAG system built around more than 568,000 Amazon product reviews. Compare retrieval strategies, then generate answers grounded in real evidence.

  • Built and compared BM25, dense, and hybrid retrieval.
  • BGE embeddings + FAISS for semantic retrieval, with cross-encoder reranking.
  • Evaluated with Hit Rate@K, MRR, and nDCG@10.
  • Product-level analytics and evidence-grounded answers from a locally run LLM.

Demonstrates: Information Retrieval · Evaluation · Applied NLP · RAG System Design
